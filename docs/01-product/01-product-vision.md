# Product Vision

> **Document Version:** 1.0  
> **Status:** Active  
> **Last Updated:** July 2026

## Vision

**ClashLens aims to become the most trusted decision-support platform for Clash of Clans clan leaders.**

Instead of simply displaying game statistics, ClashLens transforms live and historical clan data into meaningful insights, helping leaders make faster, more objective, and data-driven decisions.

## Mission

Help clan leaders spend less time managing their clans and more time enjoying the game.

By automating repetitive analysis and presenting clear recommendations, ClashLens enables leaders to:

- Understand the current state of their clan.
- Monitor member contributions.
- Identify problems before they affect the clan.
- Make consistent management decisions.

## Problem Statement

Managing a successful Clash of Clans clan requires constant monitoring of many different factors.

A leader often needs to manually check:

- Donations
- War participation
- Hero progression
- Player activity
- Clan Games participation
- Capital Raid contributions

The Clash of Clans game provides these statistics individually, but it does not combine them into actionable insights.

As clans grow larger, manually evaluating every member becomes time-consuming and inconsistent.

## Target Audience

### Primary Audience

Clan Leaders

People responsible for:

- Managing members
- Starting wars
- Promoting players
- Removing inactive members
- Maintaining an active and competitive clan

### Secondary Audience

Co-Leaders

People assisting with:

- War management
- Recruitment
- Member evaluation
- Daily clan administration

### Future Audience

- Competitive clans requiring advanced analytics
- Clan recruiters seeking data-driven member evaluation
- Players looking for clans that match their activity level and play style

## Phases

### Phase 1 — Live Clan Analytics

Build a modern, fast, and intuitive interface for exploring live Clash of Clans clan data.

Users should be able to search any public clan and quickly understand its current state through clear, actionable insights.

### Phase 2 — Evaluation Framework

Develop a transparent evaluation framework for assessing clan members.

The focus is on creating clear, explainable evaluation criteria rather than relying on arbitrary scoring systems.

### Phase 3 — Historical Analytics

Enable clan tracking by collecting and storing historical data over time.

This unlocks trend analysis and long-term insights that are not possible using only live API data.

### Phase 4 — Intelligent Insights

Provide data-driven recommendations, including:

- Promotion candidates
- War lineup suggestions
- Inactive member detection
- Removal recommendations
- Clan health analysis
- Weekly and monthly reports

## Product Principles

### 1. Transparency

Every score and recommendation must be explainable.

Users should always understand why a recommendation was made.

### 2. Objectivity

Recommendations should be based on measurable data rather than personal opinion.

### 3. Simplicity

Present complex information in a way that is easy to understand.

Avoid unnecessary clutter.

### 4. Accuracy

Never display information that cannot be supported by available data.

If historical data is unavailable, ClashLens should not attempt to estimate it.

### 5. Trust

Users should feel confident that every recommendation is supported by real evidence.

## Product Positioning

ClashLens is:

- A clan analytics platform.
- A clan management assistant.
- A decision-support system.
- A historical analytics platform.
- A productivity tool for clan leaders.

It is **not**:

- A Clash of Clans replacement.
- A game modification.
- A cheating tool.
- A bot.
- A statistics website that simply mirrors in-game data.

## Unique Value Proposition

Most Clash of Clans tools focus on displaying data.

ClashLens focuses on helping leaders answer questions such as:

- Who should participate in the next war?
- Who deserves promotion?
- Which members require attention?
- Why is the clan performing poorly?
- What should the leader do next?

The goal is to transform data into decisions.

## Long-Term Vision

ClashLens should evolve through four stages.

```text
Data
    ↓
Information
    ↓
Insights
    ↓
Recommendations
```

Eventually, a clan leader should be able to rely on ClashLens as an intelligent assistant for managing every aspect of their clan.

## Success Criteria

ClashLens will be considered successful when:

- Leaders spend less time manually reviewing members.
- Recommendations are trusted and consistently followed.
- Historical analytics provide meaningful long-term insights.
- Clan management becomes faster, simpler, and more objective.

## Guiding Principle

> **The Clash of Clans API provides data.**
>
> **ClashLens provides understanding.**
